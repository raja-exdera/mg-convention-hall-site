02 / About The Venue

Where tradition meets comfort.

Inspired by the meaning of Saptha Aradhana - seven acts of reverence - our venue is built to honour every celebration with grace, dignity and warmth.

From traditional weddings to intimate family gatherings and corporate events, we provide a space where memories are created with meaning.
