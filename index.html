02 / About The Venue

A destination shaped around the whole occasion.

M.G Convention Hall, Bidarahalli is presented through architectural and interior concepts as a complete venue experience — bringing together a convention hall, landscaped outdoor areas, dining, room stay, reception, conference and lounge spaces.

The design language combines contemporary architecture, warm timber, natural light, soft neutral finishes and greenery.
